## Final team checklist

- [ ] All four members can launch the integrated application and explain their own module.
- [ ] Each member demonstrates all four CRUD operations using synthetic test data.
- [ ] Source-to-output verification reruns on the exact submitted version.
- [ ] Every listed route and schema field matches the current implementation.
- [ ] Student self-check and two genuine peer annotations are complete.
- [ ] Tutor approval is recorded with its real date and decision.
- [ ] Client acceptance and a timed staff trial are completed or honestly identified as pending.
- [ ] Personal portfolio, resume, cover letter and LinkedIn content reflect actual work.
- [ ] No company data appears in public posts, repositories or portfolio screenshots.
- [ ] LMS due dates, required naming, format and submission receipts are checked.
